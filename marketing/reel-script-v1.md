# Hookpost reel — "Ek post, bees jagah"

**Length:** 30–35s · **Format:** 1080×1920 · **For:** Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X

Every number below is from `pricing.ts` and the live product. Nothing is rounded up.

---

## The one rule for this cut

**Do not show Instagram or Facebook being connected.** The Meta app is still
Unpublished, so a real viewer who tries it will fail at exactly the step the reel
promised. Demo **X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Discord, Telegram, Slack** — those work today.
Swap Instagram back in the day the Meta app goes live.

## Hook alternates (test these, shot 1 only)

The first two seconds decide everything. Shoot all three, run whichever holds.

- **A — the number.** Screen fills with `₹599`, then `vs`, then `₹2,400` (what four per-channel seats cost elsewhere). VO: "Yehi kaam, chauthai paise mein."
- **B — the confession.** Straight to camera, no edit: "Maine ek social media scheduler banaya. Kyunki jo the, unme se koi apne server pe nahi chalta tha." Slower, more honest, works better for X and LinkedIn.
- **C — the pain.** Phone screen, notification: "Your scheduled post failed." VO: "Ye dekha hai kabhi?" Then cut to the composer.

## What NOT to say

- **Not "30+ platforms".** The add-channel screen shows 20. `pricing.ts` carries a
  comment about exactly this: quoting 30 reads as a promise the screen does not keep.
- **Not "unlimited".** Nothing here is unlimited. Free is 2 channels / 30 posts,
  Standard is 5 / 500.
- **Not a free-trial claim.** There is a free *plan*, which is a different thing and
  a better one — no card, no countdown.
- **No Instagram or Facebook** until the Meta app is published.

---

## Where to post it

Post it from Hookpost itself and say so in the caption — a scheduler that schedules
its own launch reel is the proof, not a claim. That also gives a real screen-recording
for the *next* reel.

Boost only after the organic cut clears ~1,000 views on its own. The last ₹738
bought 10,061 impressions, 470 landing-page views and one signup who never connected
a channel. Paying to amplify a video nobody watched organically repeats that.
